Source health — which feeds are actually working
A source marked red returned nothing on the last run. That is usually a wrong URL or slug in config/sources.yaml, not an outage.
What the funding label means. Every listing is read for phrases that say whether the money is there, whether it needs an employer at home to release you, and whether it is closed to your passport. The phrases that produced each verdict are on the card under "why this label", so a wrong call is one line to fix in config/phd.yaml.

Funding not stated is not the same as unfunded. Dutch and Swedish doctoral positions are employment contracts and their adverts often never mention money. Those sources are marked salaried at the source rather than by keyword. Anything genuinely silent is labelled "funding not stated" and shown, never dropped.

The gap this page does not cover. UK and Irish studentships are advertised on FindAPhD, which publishes no feed. Set a saved search there by hand and treat it as a second source.
